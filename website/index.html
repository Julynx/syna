Why Syna?

Sandboxed by Design

Every command runs inside an isolated container pod with full root access — and your machine out of reach.

A Complete Toolset

Web search and browser use (local, no API key), image understanding and generation, speech tools, and precise line-number file editing.

Made for Long Tasks

Automatic context management with a high cache hit rate, every output saved to a file, and a verifiable chain of custody from source to deliverable.

Honest Reporting

It says exactly what happened and what it couldn't verify — hardened against prompt injection and false-flag attacks.

Voice Mode

Talk to Syna and hear its answers read out loud, with transcription and synthesis built in.

A Beautiful UI

Frosted glass, sound effects, and a Y2K-video-player-style visualizer that reacts to what the agent is doing.

Ready to start?

Two minutes of setup, then give Syna something hard.
